## Executive summary

The manuscript proves three structurally interesting results — a constructive bit-by-bit lift, positive asymptotic density, and full factor complexity $p_W(n) = 2^n$ — for a family of residues arising from the parallel reduction of the $3x-1$ map. The central proofs (parity lemma 2.3, lift theorems 3.1/3.2, factor complexity 5.1) are written carefully and, on independent re-verification, are correct. However, the appendix table and the definition of $\mathcal{O}_L$ are mutually inconsistent (the table counts the general $v_2(r-1) \ge 1$ case while Definition 2.2 restricts to $v_2(r-1) = 1$), and the $G_0 / G_{\ne 0}$ columns of the appendix table are numerically wrong starting at $L = 6$ — independently verified by running the supporting code at the strict and general definitions and by direct hand calculation of the three $L=6$ obstructions. Two further proof gaps (case completeness in Theorem 6.2; uniqueness in Lemma 6.1) and several AMS-style omissions (no MSC2020, no contact email, no acknowledgement/funding line) need to be addressed before submission.

## Findings

### Critical

- **ID** — C-01
- **Location** — `obstructions.tex:370–375` (Definition 2.2) vs `obstructions.tex:970–985` (Appendix table) and `obstructions.tex:642–650` (density table in §4).
- **Issue** — Definition 2.2 fixes $\mathcal{O}_L := \{r \in \{1,\ldots,2^L\} : r \equiv 3 \pmod 4,\, v_2(r-1) = 1,\, X_{\mathrm{end}}(r,L) = 1\}$, i.e. $v = 1$ strictly. Section 2.8 then says "We carry $v = 1$ for concreteness" without redefining the set. But the tabulated values $|\mathcal{O}_L|$ in both tables count the *general* case $v \ge 1$. Independent enumeration (script `/tmp/list_obstr.py`, see below) shows: at $L=8$ the table reports $|\mathcal{O}_8| = 19$, but the strict definition gives $18$ (the missing element is $r = 157$ with $v_2(156) = 2$). At $L = 10$ the table reports $91$; strict gives $82$; nine "extra" residues (157, 221, 413, 437, 605, 669, 733, 893, 925) all have $v = 2$.
- **Why it matters** — The density Theorem 1.2's headline lower bound $c_W \ge 7556/65536 \approx 0.115$ is anchored at $L_0 = 16$ using the general count. Under the definition as written, the strict count at the same level is smaller, and the bound has to be re-derived (or, much better, the definition has to be widened explicitly to $v \ge 1$ — Section 2.8 already hints that the general analysis is "identical with $v$ replacing $1$ throughout"). A referee will not accept a paper whose headline numerical bound disagrees with the set defined in §2.
- **Suggestion** — Either (a) keep Definition 2.2 strict and re-run the enumeration with the strict filter (totals will drop), or (b) widen Definition 2.2 explicitly to "$r$ odd with $v_2(r-1) \ge 1$" and update the prose in §2.2 and §2.8 to match. Option (b) is closer to the manuscript's intent given the appendix table; the strict-vs-general remark in §2.8 should then be folded directly into the definition.

   At $L = 6$, hand-calculation confirms: $r = 19$ gives terminal $(c, d) = (4, -1)$ → shift index $a = 1$; $r = 27$ gives $(1, 0)$ → $a = 0$; $r = 59$ gives $(4, -1)$ → $a = 1$. So the correct split at $L=6$ is one $G_0$ and two $G_1$, *not* three $G_0$ as in row 1 of the table.
- **Why it matters** — Three independent claims rely on this table: Corollary 3.3 (strict lift balance $g_L = 2 g_{L-1} + h_{L-1}$), Theorem 6.4 (series representation), and the prose around §6.3 that uses these counts as empirical confirmation. Theorem 6.4's series, evaluated from the corrected atom counts, should reconcile with $\rho_L = |\mathcal{O}_L|/2^L$ at every $L$.

### Major

- **ID** — M-01
- **Location** — `obstructions.tex:773–800` (proof of Theorem 6.2, "No shift-zero atoms").
- **Issue** — The proof splits into Case A ($V_K^{(J)} + v_K^{(J)} \ge L$) and Case B ($V_K^{(J-1)} + v_K^{(J-1)} \in [L-1, L)$), but the case conditions reference different indices ($J$ vs $J-1$) and are not visibly exhaustive. Specifically, the scenario where the K-track does not drive termination at level $L$ (i.e. the I-track terminates instead) is not flagged. A reader has to fill in that when $v_K^{(J)} < L - V_K^{(J)}$, the I-track condition $v_I^{(J)} \ge (L-1) - V_I^{(J)}$ must hold (otherwise $J$ would not be the termination index), and that this — combined with the synchronisation $v_I^{(J)} = v_K^{(J)} + 2a$ — gives the desired Case B condition. This bridge is not in the manuscript.
- **Why it matters** — Theorem 6.2 is the load-bearing input for Theorem 6.4 (series representation of $c_W$). A referee will flag the case analysis as incomplete until the K-track / I-track stop logic is made explicit.
- **Suggestion** — Add one or two sentences before the case split that establish: "Since the reduction at $(r, L)$ terminates at $J$, at least one of the K- or I-track satisfies the stop condition there. By Corollary 2.4 (simultaneous stop), if $X_{\mathrm{end}} = 1$ both stop at $J$. Therefore one of (A) $V_K^{(J)} + v_K^{(J)} \ge L$ (K-track drives), or (B) the I-track drives — equivalently $v_I^{(J)} \ge (L-1) - V_I^{(J)}$ — which via $v_I^{(J)} = v_K^{(J)} + 2a = v_K^{(J)}$ (using $a=0$ in this proof) and $V_K = V_I + 1$ rearranges to $V_K^{(J-1)} + v_K^{(J-1)} \in [L-1, L)$." Then the cases are visibly a partition.

- **ID** — M-02
- **Location** — `obstructions.tex:759–765` (proof of Lemma 6.1, atom decomposition).
- **Issue** — The proof asserts "every $r \in \mathcal{O}_L$ has a unique smallest $L_0 \ge 6$ with $r \bmod 2^{L_0} \in \mathcal{A}_{L_0}$" but does not prove uniqueness. The lift theorem 1.1 establishes that every atom propagates forward; uniqueness of the *backwards trace* (descend from $r$ at $L$ to the smallest level where the reduction first lands in $\mathcal{O}$) is a separate claim. It is in fact true — the smallest $L_0$ is determined by the largest $L_0$ for which $r \bmod 2^{L_0 - 1} \notin \mathcal{O}_{L_0 - 1}$, and the lift theorem ensures that all larger levels are then occupied — but this needs one or two sentences in the proof.
- **Why it matters** — Lemma 6.1 is the foundation of the atom decomposition $|\mathcal{O}_L| = \sum_{L_0 = 6}^L |\mathcal{A}_{L_0}| \cdot 2^{L - L_0}$, which Theorem 6.4 in turn uses. As written, the proof is closer to a hint than a proof.
- **Suggestion** — Add: "Existence: starting from $r$ at level $L$, descend by truncation $r \bmod 2^{L-1}, r \bmod 2^{L-2}, \ldots$; by Theorem 1.1 each successor is in $\mathcal{O}$ until the first $L_0$ where it leaves — that level $L_0$ is the atomic anchor. Uniqueness: if $r$ traced back to two distinct atomic anchors, the larger one would not be the smallest $L_0$ — contradiction with the definition."

## Recommendation

Submit-blocking work, in priority order:

1. **C-01 + C-02 + C-03 + C-04 together.** Decide once whether $\mathcal{O}_L$ is defined with $v = 1$ strictly or $v \ge 1$ generally, update Definition 2.2 to match, regenerate the appendix table from the code with the chosen definition, and verify that every row of the table satisfies the recurrence $g_L = 2 g_{L-1} + h_{L-1}$ and $|\mathcal{O}_L| = 2 |\mathcal{O}_{L-1}| + |\mathcal{A}_L^{G_{\ne 0}}|$. This is roughly a half-day of work but it touches the headline numerical claim and the appendix's empirical confirmation, so it must be done with care.
2. **M-01 + M-02.** Two short proof patches (each adds 2–4 lines) to close the case-completeness gap in Theorem 6.2 and the uniqueness gap in Lemma 6.1.
3. **M-03 + M-04.** Add AMS metadata (MSC2020, keywords, email, acknowledgement) and one or two missing canonical references (Lind–Marcus at minimum). Trivial mechanical work.
4. The Minor and Note items can be cleared during proof-reading.

After (1)–(3) the manuscript should be ready for submission to a Collatz-friendly venue (Discrete Mathematics, Integers, J Combin Number Theory, or an AMS journal — pick by audience). The result is genuinely interesting and the proofs hold up; the blockers are presentational and numerical-consistency issues, not gaps in the underlying theory.
