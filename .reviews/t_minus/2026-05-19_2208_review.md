## Executive summary

The manuscript establishes three structural results for the family $\mathcal{O}_L \subseteq \{1, \ldots, 2^L\}$ of obstruction residues for the $3x-1$ map: a constructive lift (Theorem 1.2), positive asymptotic density $c_W \in [3/64, 1]$ (Theorem 1.3), and full factor complexity $p_W(n) = 2^n$ (Theorem 1.4). The technical heart is the parity lemma (Lemma 2.3) and the resulting shift-$a$ normal form, which then drives the symmetry table in §3.3, the atom decomposition (Lemma 6.1), the no-shift-zero-atom result (Theorem 6.2), and the series representation of $c_W$ (Theorem 6.4). The mathematics is clean, internally consistent, and the smoke-tests confirm every empirically checkable theorem (lift at 100 % through $L_0 = 12$; $p_W(n) = 2^n$ with measured slope $\log p_W(n)/n = 0.6931 \approx \log 2$; ATOMAR-$G_0$ at $L = 10$; bidirectional $X$-criterion sharp through $L = 9$). The findings below concern writing, bibliographic hygiene, and a small number of unargued proof steps in §3.2. None of them block publication once addressed.

### Major

- **M-01** — **Theorem 1.3: statement and proof are not stitched together explicitly.**
  - **Location:** `obstructions.tex:199-207` (statement), `obstructions.tex:587-596` (proof).
  - **Issue:** The statement asserts $c_W \in [3/64, 1]$ and, separately, $c_W \ge |\mathcal{O}_{L_0}|/2^{L_0}$ for every $L_0 \ge 2$. The proof (lines 587–596) establishes only the second clause (monotone convergence). The numerical value $3/64$ is the special case $L_0 = 6$ with $|\mathcal{O}_6| = 3$, but neither the proof nor the surrounding text *concludes* the first clause from the second. The reader is asked to combine the monotone-convergence result with the introductory enumeration $\mathcal{O}_6 = \{19, 27, 59\}$ (line 164–165) on their own.
  - **Why it matters:** A referee will read the theorem statement, then the proof, and ask "where did $3/64$ come from?" The answer is in the prose two paragraphs earlier, but the proof itself does not call it out.
  - **Suggestion:** Add one closing sentence to the proof block: "Specialising to $L_0 = 6$ and using $|\mathcal{O}_6| = 3$ (direct enumeration; see §1.2 and Appendix A), $c_W \ge 3/64$." Alternatively, weaken the theorem statement to $c_W \in [\rho_{L_0}, 1]$ for any $L_0 \ge 2$ and discuss the value $3/64$ outside the theorem.

- **M-02** — **Proof of Theorem 3.2: the discrepancy formula is stated without an induction step.**
  - **Location:** `obstructions.tex:539-544`.
  - **Issue:** The proof asserts, "As long as the $v$-values coincide between the two reductions (which is the case while $V_K^{(j)} < L_0$ and $V_I^{(j)} < L_0 - 1$), $\Delta_K^{(j)} = 2^{L_0 - V_K^{(j)}} \cdot 3^j$, $\Delta_I^{(j)} = 2^{L_0 - 1 - V_I^{(j)}} \cdot 3^j$." Two non-trivial claims are bundled into a single parenthetical: (a) that the $v$-values coincide under that hypothesis, and (b) that the discrepancies have the stated form. Neither is proved.
  - **Why it matters:** (a) is the load-bearing assumption — if it fails, the whole stop-type SS branch of the proof breaks. (b) is an inductive consequence of (a) plus the basic step, but the induction is not written out.
  - **Suggestion:** Add a short sub-lemma or two-paragraph block: prove (a) by observing that as long as $V_K^{(j)} < L_0$, the modulus $2^{L_0 + 1 - V_K^{(j)}}$ at level $L_0+1$ is at least twice the modulus at level $L_0$, so $a_{K,-}^{(j)} \equiv a_{K,+}^{(j)} \pmod{2^{L_0 - V_K^{(j)} + 1}}$, hence $v_2(3 a_{K,-}^{(j)} - 1) = v_2(3 a_{K,+}^{(j)} - 1)$. Then derive (b) inductively from $\Delta_K^{(j+1)} = (3 \Delta_K^{(j)})/2^{v_K^{(j)}}$.

- **M-03** — **"By the carry identity" — an unnamed identity is invoked.**
  - **Location:** `obstructions.tex:555-557` (stop-type EE branch of Theorem 3.2's proof).
  - **Issue:** The proof says "$v_2(A_{K,-}^{(J)}) \ge v_K^{(J)} + 1 = L_0 + 1 - V_K^{(J)}$ by the carry identity." The identity in question is the well-known $v_2(a + b) > \min(v_2 a, v_2 b)$ when $v_2 a = v_2 b$ and the leading bits cancel, but it is referenced by a name (`the carry identity`) that has not been introduced in the paper.
  - **Why it matters:** A reader who does not already know the argument cannot reconstruct it; a referee will mark this.
  - **Suggestion:** Either spell out the inequality with one line of arithmetic ("since $v_2(A_{K,+}^{(J)}) = v_K^{(J)} = v_2(\Delta_K^{(J)})$ and the two summands are equal modulo $2^{v_K^{(J)} + 1}$, $v_2(A_{K,-}^{(J)}) = v_2(A_{K,+}^{(J)} + \Delta_K^{(J)} \cdot c) \ge v_K^{(J)} + 1$"), or introduce a short lemma earlier and cite it by label.

- **M-04** — **`Eliahou1993` is an orphan in `references.bib`.**
  - **Location:** `references.bib:11-18` (entry); never cited in `obstructions.tex`.
  - **Issue:** The entry is defined but not `\cite`d. Either the citation belongs in the manuscript (Eliahou's lower bounds on nontrivial cycle lengths are directly relevant to §8.2 "Bridges to nontrivial cycles") or the entry should be removed.
  - **Why it matters:** Bibliographic hygiene; a referee will notice; the introduction reads cleaner once the question is settled.
  - **Suggestion:** If §8.2 is to discuss the cycle-length question seriously, add one sentence citing Eliahou and Belaga–Mignotte; otherwise delete the entry from `references.bib`.

## Strengths (decision-relevant)

- **The mathematical core is original and well-organised.** The shift-$a$ normal form (Lemma 2.3 / §2.5) is a clean structural observation; the entire paper flows from it without ad-hoc constructions.
- **Numerical claims are reproducibly verified.** The reproduction package is self-contained, runs in pure Python with no third-party dependencies, and its conclusions match the manuscript at every level the reviewer tested.
- **The series representation of $c_W$ (Theorem 6.4) is a genuinely useful structural result.** It reduces the open problem (the exact value of $c_W$) to a single quantitative bound on $|\mathcal{A}_L^{G_{\ne 0}}|$, which is a sharp statement of what is actually unknown.
- **The corollary on substitution-shift rigidity (Corollary 5.3) lands a sharp negative result** — three classical descriptions of $(\mathcal{O}_L)$ are ruled out at once via a single entropy argument — and connects the obstruction family to the symbolic-dynamics literature in a substantive way.

## Recommendation

The paper is close to publication-ready and should be returned to the author with **minor revisions**. Concrete priority order:

1. **Tighten the proof of Theorem 3.2** (M-02, M-03): add the missing induction for the discrepancy formula, and either name or spell out the carry identity. This is the single place in the paper where a careful referee will pause.
2. **Fix the bibliography hygiene** (M-04, m-04): decide on `Eliahou1993` (cite or drop) and add DOIs to `Tao2022` / `Devyatov2015`.
3. **Stitch the Theorem 1.3 statement to its proof** (M-01) with one closing sentence.
4. **Cosmetic cleanup** (m-01, m-02, m-03, m-05, m-06, m-07, m-08, m-09): definition deduplication, the `ATOMAR-$G_0$` German relic, the unlabelled entropy corollary, the overfull `\hbox`es, the scope tightening, the explicit verification pointer in the Theorem 1.4 proof, the author block, and the em-dashes in the appendix table.
5. **Pre-submission code cleanup** (m-11, m-12, n-03): each script should print one final "verified" / "failed" line; remove the dead `trace_X_general`; update stale docstrings; decide what to do with the private-repo provenance pointer in the README.

Estimated work to publication readiness: **half a day to two days**, depending on how thoroughly the author wants to expand the proof of Theorem 3.2 and how many of the optional bibliographic suggestions (m-10, n-04) they choose to act on.
